# Postcue for Cursor

Plan social posts from Cursor. [Postcue](https://getpostcue.com) reminds you on your phone at the time you set, with
the frames saved to Photos and the caption on the clipboard, and opens the network's app. You add the music and post
it yourself. Postcue never posts for you and never asks for your social network passwords.

This plugin adds:

- **The Postcue MCP server** (`https://getpostcue.com/mcp`): list your workspaces and accounts, schedule, move,
  change or cancel posts, upload frames, and see which account has gone quiet.
- **A `schedule-posts` skill**, so the agent knows how to turn a `POST.md` and its images in your repo into a
  scheduled post.

## Setup

1. Install the Postcue app on your iPhone and sign in with your email.
2. Install this plugin from the Cursor Marketplace.
3. The first time a Postcue tool runs, Cursor opens a sign-in page. Use the same email as in the app and enter the
   code it sends you.

Or add only the server, without the plugin:

[![Add Postcue to Cursor](https://cursor.com/deeplink/mcp-install-dark.svg)](https://cursor.com/install-mcp?name=postcue&config=eyJ1cmwiOiJodHRwczovL2dldHBvc3RjdWUuY29tL21jcCJ9)

## Try it

- "Set up a Postcue workspace for my bakery, Sweet Crumbs, with Instagram @sweetcrumbs, London time."
- "Schedule the carousel in `posts/croissants/` for Friday 8:00 on Instagram, caption from its POST.md."
- "Which of my accounts has gone quiet? Suggest a post for it."
- "Move everything scheduled this weekend to Monday evening."

## What it can reach

Only your own Postcue data: workspaces, accounts, posts and their frames. No tool touches a social network.
